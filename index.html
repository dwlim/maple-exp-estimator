How this is calculated & data assumptions

How this is calculated

EXP/hour uses OSMS map spawn data, party clear speed, and your uptime setting. The party is modeled as roughly half your selected job and half Warriors.

Job estimates

  • Cleric: Holy Arrow, speed 6; half the melee movement loss and 50% bonus damage against bundled undead and holy-weak monsters.
  • Fire/Poison: Fire Arrow, speed 6; half the melee movement loss and 50% bonus damage against bundled fire-weak monsters.
  • Ice/Lightning: Cold Beam, speed 6; half the melee movement loss and 50% bonus damage against bundled ice-weak monsters.
  • Assassin: Lucky 7, speed 3, 50% movement estimate. Bowman: Arrow Blow, speed 6, 75% movement estimate. Bandit: Savage Blow, speed 7, full melee movement estimate.

Combat and costs

  • WDEF uses a representative, unscrolled, level-appropriate armor set for each job family. It excludes scrolls, capes, shields, accessories, and bonus stats. Physical avoidance estimates contact exposure from spawn spacing, density, monster size, speed, and jumping behavior; it adjusts HP potion cost only.
  • MP cost assumes one skill cast per attack: Power Strike 6, Arrow Blow 14, Lucky Seven 8, Savage Blow 16, Holy Arrow 14, and elemental mage skills 20 MP.

Data & assumptions

Monster HP, EXP, and map population come from OSMS Data Explorer. Map results use Victoria Island spawn data. Maps without usable monster spawns or with zero total EXP are excluded.

Character model

  • Job DPS uses estimated clean-gear stats and a representative attack skill. It does not model misses, monster defense, exact mastery, multi-target attacks, or equipment variation.
  • Movement uses a parallel-area route estimate. It does not model exact paths, platforms, ladders, drops, or party positioning.
  • Potion costs use estimated character WDEF and OSMS monster physical attack data. Incoming physical damage uses the Classic defense mitigation formula: monster attack × 100 / (100 + WDEF), with a minimum of 1 damage. The WDEF baseline is unscrolled gear, so actual damage and potion use can vary substantially with upgrades.
