Model: a full map clear takes total monster kills × shots × seconds per shot ÷ party size. The map cycle is limited by whichever is slower: clearing the map or the longest monster respawn. EXP is divided evenly among the party and reduced by uptime. This is a planning estimate, not a measured in-game rate.
