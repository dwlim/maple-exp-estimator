How this is calculated

The rate uses OSMS’s weighted EXP model with your party’s clear speed: clear time is total map HP ÷ (average DPS per member × party size), and the cycle cannot be faster than OSMS’s 7.56-second default spawn cycle. Party size divides the map total evenly between characters.

Higher DPS increases EXP until the party reaches the OSMS spawn-rate ceiling. Spearman mode estimates sustained Power Strike damage using clean base stats, the OSMS spear progression, weapon speed, and a level-scaled skill rank. The movement estimate models parallel area coverage: it partitions spawn points into party-sized bands along the map’s dominant dimension, uses local nearest-spawn travel inside each area, adds a modest area-size coverage penalty, and uses the slowest area at 180 px/s. This rewards maps that can be split efficiently without forcing an overly long sequential route. Details mode lets you enter DPS directly. Uptime scales the result to account for other downtime. Potion damage uses OSMS PADamage reduced by estimated player WDEF. The estimate assumes clean Spearman armor, with WDEF growing from 4 at level 10 to about 154 at level 70, and uses max(1, PADamage − WDEF) for each physical hit. Details mode still uses entered DPS, while character level controls the WDEF estimate. Potion cost is estimated damage divided by 2 (1 meso = 2 HP).

Monster HP, EXP, and map population defaults are from OSMS Data Explorer. Weighted EXP uses OSMS’s 7.56-second default spawn cycle. Spearman mode is a transparent clean-gear Power Strike estimate, not a measured class build. Movement loss is an approximate parallel-area route estimate from OSMS coordinates, including map size and mob count. It can split broad maps across party members, but does not model the exact player route, ladders, drops, or party positioning.
