Calculation

EXP uses OSMS spawn rates, map population, party clear speed, and uptime. Level + job estimates a party made of roughly half the selected class and half Warriors. Assassins use Lucky 7 at attack speed 3 with 50% of melee movement loss. Bowmen use Arrow Blow at attack speed 6 with 75% of melee movement loss. Bandits use Savage Blow at attack speed 7 with 100% of melee movement loss. Thieves use 72% of equivalent Warrior WDEF; manual mode uses your entered values. Physical avoidance estimates contact exposure from spawn spacing, density, monster size, speed, and jumping behavior, then adjusts potion usage only.

Data: OSMS Data Explorer. Class values are clean-gear estimates; each party is roughly half the selected class and half Warriors. Assassins use attack speed 3 with 50% of melee movement loss. Bowmen use attack speed 6 with 75% of melee movement loss. Bandits use attack speed 7 with 100% of melee movement loss; Thieves use 72% of equivalent Warrior WDEF. Movement is an approximate parallel-area route.
